One public URL, five perspectives.

What this tool does

Website Intelligence reads a public website URL, extracts its real content, and uses a large language model to produce a structured business report. Instead of generic advice, every recommendation is grounded in what the site actually says: its products, services, positioning and language. You get a single-page report that covers five perspectives on the same business, ready to share with founders, marketers or clients.

The five perspectives

Competitor vs your brand

Identifies your brand, category and services, then suggests at least three relevant competitors with strengths, weaknesses and positioning opportunities. Competitor suggestions are AI-generated and should be verified before use in strategy decks.

Top 5 search queries

Five realistic queries potential customers would type on Google, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn and X, each tagged with search intent, platform fit and target audience — with no invented search volumes.

SEO improvements

Primary, secondary and long-tail keyword recommendations, a rewritten SEO title, a human-friendly meta description and supporting content ideas such as landing pages, FAQs and blog topics.

Top 5 services

The five most important services identified from the site's own copy, each with a short description, target industry, target customer and the main benefit it delivers. Inferred items are clearly labelled.

Target audience

Primary and secondary audiences, industry segments, business types, customer profiles, geographic focus, customer needs and challenges, and recommended segments for marketing campaigns.

How it works

Paste any public URL into the form above. The service validates the address, blocks private or internal network targets (SSRF protection), fetches the HTML, strips navigation, scripts and boilerplate, and sends the cleaned text to the configured LLM with a strict JSON schema. The model's response is validated with Pydantic before it reaches you, so the output is always structured, predictable JSON rather than free-form prose. If a provider is unavailable, the service fails over to the next configured provider automatically.

Analysis typically completes in under a minute. Results are shown on this page and are also available as raw JSON from the POST /link-analysis endpoint. Competitor recommendations are suggestions, not verified market research — always confirm them against primary sources before acting on them.

Who it is for

Founders preparing a go-to-market plan, marketers building keyword and content strategies, agencies auditing a new prospect's site, and developers integrating website intelligence into their own workflows via the REST API. The interface requires no account; the API is documented at /docs.
